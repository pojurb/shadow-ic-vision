**R-025, re-scoped 2026-08-08 — the corpus, not only the matcher.** Two
independent reviews converged on a diagnosis (the system conflates *passage
found*, *passage worth reviewing*, and *evidence judged relevant*), and both
assumed without testing that the corpus contains material worth distinguishing.
A direct inspection of the live TLKM sources found otherwise. Of 51 persisted
evidence rows: ~25 come from daily market-wire round-ups (index moves, foreign
net-sell figures), ~13 from CSR and education press releases (student
programmes, village development, a 61st-anniversary item), and the only document
classified `Issuer official` is a **sustainability report** — not a financial
statement. Meanwhile **all six** TLKM official-source jobs sit at `degraded` /
`source_too_large` after 8–9 attempts, so the financial statements have never
once been read.

That reorders the remedy without overturning the diagnosis. A relevance-review
loop built on this corpus would have the user labelling ~45 market-wire and CSR
passages as irrelevant one at a time, against a corpus that changes completely
the moment the official path is repaired — and any ranking or volume
calibration made now would not survive that repair. The 88.9% figure below
therefore measures a *matcher* pointed at a corpus that mostly cannot answer the
questions asked of it; how much of it is attributable to the matcher alone is
not yet separable. **M013** repairs the official path and classifies each
assumption's source adequacy so the two causes can be told apart. The distinct
risk that some assumptions have no public source at all is recorded separately
as **R-028**; R-025 remains `Open` and untouched by M013.

**R-025, sharpened 2026-08-06.** `e8a99c3` generalized the gap the table row
below already named — "company-name tokens are deliberately not excluded" —
from the ticker and bare years to the company's full name and market, passed
in as `identity` from the thesis. A regression test proved it closes the
specific case that motivated it (a stock-index round-up matched an assumption
purely on the ticker plus "Indonesia"). That commit's own message called this
a step toward a "relevance gate," which an independent review (a second
AI collaborator) found to overclaim what shipped: token overlap decides
**whether a passage becomes Evidence at all**, not merely which candidates
are worth ranking, and identity-token exclusion does not touch the far larger
share of false positives that clear the filter on generic, non-identity
vocabulary instead.

The review's own instrumentation, replicated directly rather than taken on
trust: a real persisted quote about a sustainability award passed for the
assumption "hyperscaler capital commitments flow through NeutraDC" on the
tokens `komitmen` ("commitment"), `sebagai` ("as"), and `digital` — none an
identity token, and `tokenMatches * 3` alone (9 points) already clears the
score floor (8) without any number match. A manual audit of the live TLKM
thesis's entire retained secondary corpus (11 documents, 6 assumptions, 66
document/assumption combinations, 72 emitted candidates), judged against
"does this passage contain a proposition capable of changing evaluation of
the assigned metric or relationship," found 4 directly relevant, 4 adjacent
but insufficiently claim-specific, and 64 clearly irrelevant — an 88.9%
false-positive estimate (94.4% if adjacent is correctly treated as
insufficient). Independently eyeballed against the live database's 39
persisted secondary evidence rows during this same session: at most 2-3
plausibly relevant, the rest generic market-index commentary, CSR, awards,
and education press releases attached to unrelated assumptions — the same
picture, a different method.

`sebagai` and several other common Indonesian function words (`dengan`,
`dalam`, `pada`, `oleh`, `serta`, `juga`) are absent from `STOP_WORDS`
(`lib/research/extractors/candidate.ts`) while their rough English
equivalents (`as`, `is`, `are`) are present — a confirmed asymmetry, though
closing it alone would not resolve the finding: `komitmen` and `digital` are
not function words, they are corporate-vocabulary terms that recur across
unrelated TLKM releases regardless of subject (measured 6/7 and 6/7 documents
respectively in the sampled corpus), and no single proposed remedy (a higher
qualifying-token floor, a generic-vocabulary denylist, or TF-IDF/BM25 alone)
was judged sufficient by itself — a floor raised from 1 to 2 would still
retain 37 candidates from clearly irrelevant documents in the same corpus.
Candidate remedies of increasing scope were laid out and none has been
selected: renaming the mechanism honestly and adding the missing stop words
(low-risk hygiene); a deterministic relevance contract connecting a passage
to the measurement contract's own concept groups (entity/alias, metric or
relationship, event type) rather than arbitrary token overlap; and, only if
general paraphrase resolution is required, a governed model-based relevance
assessor — explicitly **not** authorized by
[`DEC-0016`](decisions/DEC-0016-evidence-polarity-classifier-boundary.md),
which covers polarity classification after evidence already exists, not a
relevance decision gating whether evidence is created at all; that would need
its own decision record and milestone.

`e8a99c3` itself is not reverted — the identity-token exclusion and an
unrelated exact-number-match fix (substring matching previously let an
assumption's threshold of 30 be "matched" by 130 or 2030) are both real,
narrow improvements, kept. Scope of the remedy is deliberately undecided as
of this entry — recorded so the gap and its evidence survive, not so a
specific fix is pre-approved.

## Review Rules

- Reassess likelihood, impact, mitigation, trigger, and residual risk before the
  related milestone is approved.
- Add a risk when new product, data, model, security, legal, or operational
  uncertainty becomes material.
- Accepted residual risk requires an explicit decision record from the user.
- A critical trust, privacy, or data-integrity risk blocks milestone closure and
  release until mitigated or explicitly rejected from scope.
